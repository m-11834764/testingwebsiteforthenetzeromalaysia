Let’s Decarbonise Malaysia

We are raising awareness among Malaysians to fight climate change effectively without negatively affecting our quality of life. We lay out solutions to decarbonization across various sectors.

Learn more

The Problem

Malaysia is far behind in meaningful climate action. This is due to its heavy reliance on fossil fuels for energy and transportation. Besides, cities in Malaysia are designed in a car-centric way. Because of unreliable public transportation in some areas, private vehicles continue to be the first choice of commuters.

Our Agenda to Decarbonise Malaysia

We will not reach net-zero if we rely only on individual action without systemic changes. We are building a framework that if applied, will make a low-carbon life possible with little to no inconvenience.
